Your Privacy

AnatomyLens · Last updated: September 24, 2026

AnatomyLens is a free educational study app designed with privacy in mind.

  • Camera images and video are processed in real time on your device. They are not recorded, saved to files, or uploaded by AnatomyLens.
  • AnatomyLens does not collect personal information for the developer or any third party.
  • AnatomyLens does not sell, share, or track personal information.
  • Camera access is used only to provide the anatomy visualization features, after you tap Scan and grant camera permission.

Temporary processing memory

Real-time visualization requires camera pixels and estimated landmarks in temporary device memory. Small recent frames and landmark histories help keep the illustration stable during brief detection loss and close-ups. The app clears its recent-frame buffer and application-level landmark history when scanning stops, the camera changes, capture is released, or the app goes into the background. The on-device MediaPipe tracker may keep transient inference state in memory while its model remains loaded; it is not written to persistent storage and is discarded when the app process closes. This is not a photo or video recording feature.

Information we do not collect

AnatomyLens has no account, login, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting service, cloud storage, or upload feature. It does not request your name, email, contacts, location, microphone, or photo library. It does not set tracking cookies or store personal information in browser storage.

Network and platform services

The installed iPhone app includes its tracking runtime and model files. Camera frames and landmarks are processed locally, with no remote anatomy API. If you use a hosted web version or visit a support or privacy website, its hosting provider receives ordinary web requests, including your IP address and browser information. Hosting logs and retention depend on that website's configuration; this notice does not claim those providers collect nothing. Apple's App Store and device diagnostics are governed by Apple's policies and your device settings.

A screenshot or screen recording that you choose to make using your device is controlled by you and the operating system. AnatomyLens does not create or upload such recordings.

Educational limitation

Overlays are generic educational illustrations estimated from visible landmarks. They do not see beneath skin, reconstruct your actual bones or muscles, diagnose a condition, or provide medical advice.

Support and changes

For privacy questions, use the publisher's support contact on the AnatomyLens App Store page. We will update this notice and the App Store privacy information before releasing a feature that changes data handling.
