# 3D skull asset

`skull.glb` is ScatteringSkull from KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets.

- Creator: Vladimir Petkovic
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
- Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/ScatteringSkull
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

The app replaces the source's demonstration scattering material with ivory shading,
normalizes geometry, and applies an approximate jaw deformation. This is a generic
educational skull mesh, not a reconstruction of the camera user's bones or teeth.
