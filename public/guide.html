How to use AnatomyLens

Use the camera to explore an educational illustration of hand and forearm anatomy. The illustration follows visible landmarks; it is an estimate, not an image of structures beneath your skin.

Start a scan

  1. Choose Rear or Front using the camera switch beside Scan hand.
  2. Tap Scan hand and allow camera access when your iPhone asks.
  3. Hold your hand in view with room for the wrist and forearm. Good lighting and a steady view help tracking.

Explore the anatomy

  • Choose Bones or Muscles in the bottom controls.
  • Tap a visible bone or anatomy label to open its information.
  • Open Settings to change label detail. In Bones, Wrist labels can be Off, A–H, or A–H + names; tap an A–H label to learn its name.
  • In Muscles, use Settings to choose Palm or Back and adjust muscle labels.

Keep the forearm aligned

When the app shows Forearm preview · tap Align elbow, tap Align elbow, then tap your elbow in the camera view while your hand is visible. Choose Auto elbow to return to automatic placement. An estimated or pinned elbow may not match your actual elbow exactly.

Tracking and camera controls

  • If tracking is uncertain or disappears, move your hand and forearm back into view and hold still briefly. Close-up anatomy may be marked estimated while the app uses recent visual evidence.
  • Use the camera switch to change between Front and Rear. Switching cameras clears tracking history so the new view can be recognized.
  • Tap Stop to end scanning and release the camera. Tap Scan hand to start again.

AnatomyLens is for study and general education. Its illustrations can be incomplete or inaccurate and are not medical imaging, diagnosis, or medical advice. See Privacy and data use for how camera data is handled.
